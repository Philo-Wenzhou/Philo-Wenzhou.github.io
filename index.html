Selected work

01

Operational wildfire risk

Developing fuel-state modelling and early warning systems to support operational wildfire risk assessment and decision-making.

I lead technical work on distribution-line wildfire risk, drawing on a review of Chinese national standards. My work spans multi-timelag dead-fuel moisture and temperature, integrated risk rules, and decision-support cards.

Research interests: model validation, transfer across scales, and how uncertainty affects risk ranking and action selection.

Explore the public FWI repository
02

Earth observation workflows

Building satellite observation workflows with quality control for environmental monitoring.

My work involves processing and applying observations from sources including FY-4B and MODIS. I am interested in how clouds, observation conditions and data quality affect monitoring results.

Explore the public FY4 repository
03

Ecosystem processes

Using field data and statistical ecology to better understand ecosystem processes and environmental change.

My MSc research examined relationships between plant- and microbial-derived soil carbon and grassland ecosystem multifunctionality, combining field sampling, laboratory analysis and statistical modelling.
